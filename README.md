# 🛒 Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Executive Summary
An end-to-end retail store analytics project evaluating net sales, profit margins, discounting impact, customer segmentation, and product return vulnerabilities across regions.

## 🔑 Key Insights & Analytics
- **Sales & Net Profit:** Achieved **$2.12M** in Net Sales and **$263.18K** in Net Profit (**12.44% Margin**) across 5,009 orders.
- **Geographic Concentration:** ~50% of sales originate from 5 major states, led by the **West Region ($0.82M)**.
- **80/20 Pareto Customer Rule:** Top **20% of customers** account for **$216.61K (82%)** of total net profit.
- **Discounting Compression:** Average discount of **15.62%** negatively affects low-margin categories like Tables (**-8.78% Profit Margin**).
- **Return Rate Vulnerability:** Overall return rate is **5.91%**, with West Region spiking to **11.73%**, specifically in Machines and Tables.
